# Coral Generation Mechanism

This mod enhances coral generation and revival mechanisms.

## Bone Meal Coral Generation

::: tip
Using bone meal in water has a chance to generate coral! Requires the player to have Conduit Power effect.
:::

### Trigger Conditions

- Player has Conduit Power effect
- Use bone meal in water
- Water nearby

### Particle Effects

Coral particle effects appear when using bone meal.

## Coral Revival Mechanism

When placing dead coral, if the following conditions are met, the coral will automatically revive:

- Player has Conduit Power effect
- Water nearby the placed dead coral

## Conduit Power Effect

Conduit Power is a buff effect obtained by placing a conduit.

### Effect Levels

| Level | Effect Range |
|-------|--------------|
| I | 16 blocks |
| II | 24 blocks |
| III | 32 blocks |
